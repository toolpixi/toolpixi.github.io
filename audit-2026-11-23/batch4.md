# ToolPixi Worldwide Search-Demand Audit — Batch 4 of 5
## Countdowns, clocks, text/dev tools, generators (55 tools)

Compiled 2026-11-23. Method: for each tool, the main keyword a person would type on Google, and the best **worldwide monthly search volume** found in public sources (Semrush domain-overview "Top Organic Keywords" tables, which show global volume; Keyword Planner figures republished by seodata.dev / kwstrends.com; Ahrefs figures republished by unanswered.io; published keyword tables on GitHub and tool sites). Where a source only publishes a US figure, that is stated in the row — it is a floor, not the global total. Volumes from Semrush/KP are 12-month rolling averages, so the "1–2yr pattern" column judges the keyword on that long window: established tool keywords with stable 12-month averages across multiple years of these tables = **sustained**; holiday countdown phrases that spike for a few weeks each year and sit near zero the rest = **seasonal**. "NO PUBLIC DATA" means at least two source types (Semrush overview tables, KP-based publishers, Ahrefs-based publishers, keyword-guide tables, dedicated-site traffic) were tried and no volume figure for that phrase is published anywhere found — per the brief, that is treated as effectively near-zero demand for that exact phrasing.

Notes:
- Every countdown keyword is **seasonal** by nature: the 12-month average hides a spike concentrated in the weeks before the holiday and near-zero searches the rest of the year. Only the Christmas and Thanksgiving phrasings have published volume figures; the other 16 countdown phrasings have no published figure anywhere found, so their exact-phrasing demand reads as near-zero even though the holidays themselves are heavily searched under other phrasings (e.g. "when is eid", "diwali 2026 date").
- US-only figures are marked as such (christmas countdown, days until christmas); worldwide totals for those are higher than the figure shown.
- Semrush overview "Volume" columns are global 12-month-average style figures; Keyword Planner figures are 12-month averages by definition. Both fit the owner's previous 1–2 years lens; no verdict in this file rests on a single recent month.
