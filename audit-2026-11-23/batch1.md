## Notes for the synthesis (parent agent)
- "No data" splits into two very different groups: (a) genuinely near-zero across 1–2 years — pdf-to-webp, compress-png-to-100kb; (b) real evergreen utility demand that no public source quantifies — rotate-pdf, add-page-numbers, add-text-to-pdf, add-watermark-to-pdf, image-watermark, plus image-resizer/image-cropper where the category is provably large via competitor traffic but no volume figure is published. Group (b) should NOT be read as zero demand.
- No keyword in this batch was a "recent blip": every quantified keyword shows a sustained 1–2yr pattern. Passport-photo demand is the only one with a seasonal overlay.
- Figures are the best PUBLIC figures found (kwstrends 12-mo averages preferred, Semrush global database figures, DataForSEO/Exploding Topics snippets). They are estimates from third-party panels, consistent in order of magnitude across sources where two existed (e.g. qr-code-generator 1.5M vs 2.7M; barcode-generator US 301K vs global 1.83M).
