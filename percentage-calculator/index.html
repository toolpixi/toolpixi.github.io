Percentages in real life

Discounts ("30% off"), exam scores ("45 out of 60"), price rises, salary bumps, battery drain, data usage — daily life is full of percentages, and they are genuinely confusing because the base number keeps changing. A price that rises 25% then falls 25% does not return to the start, and this calculator's change tool shows why: the second percentage acts on a different base. When in doubt, run the numbers here instead of guessing.
