How to use it

Choose your photo. Tap the box above and pick any JPG, PNG or WebP image.
Pick a target size. 50KB, 100KB, 200KB and 500KB presets cover most form limits, or type your own.
Compress and download. The tool finds the best quality that fits, then hands you the file.

Built for application forms

Exam portals, visa applications, job sites and government forms around the world reject photos that are even slightly too large — and their limits are almost always in kilobytes: 50KB for a passport-style photo, 100KB or 200KB for a scanned document. Phone photos are usually 2–8 megabytes, a hundred times too big. This compressor closes that gap in one tap, without installing an app.

How it works: the tool re-saves your photo as a JPEG, testing quality levels from high to low and measuring the real file size each time. Only if the smallest sensible quality is still too big does it gently reduce the dimensions as well. The result stays as sharp as your target size allows.
