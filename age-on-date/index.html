How to use it

Enter the date of birth.
Enter the date that matters — an admission cutoff, exam date, or any day you are checking.
Get the exact age on that date, in years, months and days, with totals.

The cutoff-date question, answered exactly

"Must be 5 years old on or before 1 September." "Under 19 on the tournament date." School admissions, exam boards, sports federations and pension rules all fix eligibility to a single date, and being a few days out changes the answer. This tool does the fiddly month-and-day borrowing for you and gives an answer you can quote with confidence — calculated privately, in your browser.
