HEIC to PNG Converter

Convert iPhone HEIC photos to lossless PNG files — the format to pick when every pixel matters and the file has to open everywhere.

Private by design: this tool runs entirely in your browser. Your files are never uploaded to a server, and nothing is stored.
Tap to choose HEIC photo(s) — or drop them here
.heic and .heif files from iPhone and iPad

HEIC decoding happens in your browser and can take a few seconds per photo on older phones. PNG files are lossless, so expect them to be much larger than the original HEIC — that is the honest price of keeping every pixel.

How to use it

Add your HEIC photo(s). Send them from your iPhone to this device first (email, USB, cloud drive), then choose them here.
Press Convert to PNG. Each photo is decoded and re-saved as a full-resolution PNG.
Download and edit anywhere. PNG opens in every editor and keeps transparency and fine detail.

When PNG is the right choice for an iPhone photo

JPG is the better everyday conversion — smaller, universal. PNG earns its place when the photo is not finished yet: screenshots and scans you plan to edit, images with text or sharp edges that JPG would smudge, and anything you want archived at full quality. Because this converter runs locally with the same decoder the HEIC to JPG tool uses, even private photos never pass through anyone's server.

HEIC is efficient; PNG is forever

Since 2017, iPhones have saved photos as HEIC by default, because the format stores a photo in roughly half the space of a JPG. The catch arrives the moment a photo leaves the Apple ecosystem: Windows PCs without the right codec, older Android phones, web upload forms and plenty of professional software still cannot open HEIC at all. JPG is the usual escape route — but when a photo matters enough to edit, print or archive, PNG is the safer destination, because PNG conversion is a one-time, lossless step: every pixel Apple captured is preserved, and the file will never degrade through later edits and saves.

That distinction decides which converter to use. Sending a snapshot to a form or a friend? JPG, smaller and universal. Preparing a photo for retouching, pulling stills for print, feeding images into design or machine-learning pipelines that demand PNG, or archiving originals you cannot reshoot? PNG, every time. PNG also future-proofs in a way HEIC does not: it is an open, thirty-year-old standard that every operating system and editor reads natively, with none of the patent and codec baggage that still trails HEIC on non-Apple platforms.

Expect big files — a lossless copy of a modern phone photo can run to ten megabytes or more — because fidelity, not size, is the point of this conversion. It all happens in your browser: your photos are decoded and re-encoded on your own device and are never uploaded to a server, which is exactly how personal photos should be handled. Convert a single frame or work through a batch; each keeps its full resolution.

Questions, answered

Why convert HEIC to PNG instead of JPG?

PNG is lossless: every pixel of the original photo is kept exactly, which suits editing, archiving and graphics work. JPG is smaller and better for sharing. Choose PNG when quality matters more than file size.

Will the PNG be bigger than the HEIC?

Usually yes, often several times bigger. HEIC is a highly efficient format; PNG trades size for perfect, widely-compatible quality.

Can I convert many photos at once?

Yes, select or drop several HEIC files and each downloads as its own PNG.

Is anything uploaded?

No. Your photos are decoded on your device by your browser. They never touch a server.
