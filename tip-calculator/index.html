How to use it

Type the bill total and how many people are sharing.
Pick a tip percentage — 15–20% is the US restaurant norm — or enter your own.
Read each person's share. The tip and total are shown too, so nobody has to trust anybody's mental maths.

Ending the table-side maths debate

Few moments are as reliably awkward as a table of friends doing long division over a restaurant bill. Tip customs vary by country and situation, but the arithmetic should not: tip on the bill, add it on, divide by people. This calculator does it in the time it takes to type the total — and because it updates live, you can test 15% against 18% and settle on a number everyone feels good about.
