How to use it

Set your range — lowest and highest numbers.
Choose quantity and options. No repeats for draws; sorting for tidy lists.
Generate. Press again for a fresh set — every draw is independent.

Fair draws, quick decisions

Raffles, classroom cold-calls, lottery-style games, picking who goes first, splitting into teams — whenever a choice must be visibly fair, a random draw ends the argument. Teachers use it to choose students, streamers to pick giveaway winners, and families to decide movie night. Because it runs locally with a fresh draw every click, nobody can claim the result was lined up in advance.
