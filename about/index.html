About ToolPixi

Free online tools with a simple promise: the job gets done in your browser, and your files never leave your device.

What ToolPixi is

ToolPixi is a growing toolbox of small, single-purpose web tools: compress a photo to the exact kilobyte size a form demands, convert an iPhone HEIC photo so it opens on Windows, merge or split a PDF, shrink a PDF to 200KB, calculate an exact age, count down to Ramadan, make a QR code, and dozens of everyday conversions in between. Every tool is free, works without an account, and is designed to finish its job in seconds.

Why browser-based matters

Most tool websites upload your file to their server, process it there, and keep you waiting — while your document sits on a stranger's machine. ToolPixi takes the opposite approach: all processing happens on your device, in the tab you already have open, using well-tested open-source engines (pdf-lib, PDF.js, heic2any and others). That means:

  • Privacy — your photos, PDFs and personal dates are never transmitted or stored.
  • Speed — no upload queue; big files do not travel over a slow connection.
  • No accounts, ever — open the page, do the job, leave.

Honest tools

Where a tool has a limitation, we say so on its page. Our PDF compression re-renders pages as images — great for shrinking files for forms, but the text is no longer selectable, and the page tells you that before you rely on it. Compressing an image to exactly 50KB sometimes means trading a little sharpness, and we show you the result before you download. We would rather be plain about trade-offs than promise magic.

What's next

ToolPixi is in active development. New tools are added regularly, and the most-requested jobs move to the front of the queue. If a tool here saved your day — or failed you — we genuinely want to hear it via the contact page.
