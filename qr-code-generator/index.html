QR Code Generator

Turn any link or text into a scannable QR code, then download a crisp PNG for printing, menus, packaging or your shop window.

How to use it

Paste your link or type your text. Website addresses work best exactly as you'd type them in a browser.
Pick a size and colours. Keep the code dark on a light background so every phone can scan it.
Download the PNG and print it, post it, or drop it into your design.

QR codes without the middleman

Many QR services quietly route your code through their servers, so the printed code stops working if they shut down or start charging. ToolPixi codes are the real thing: your link is encoded directly into the pattern, generated on your device. They never expire, work offline, and belong entirely to you — ideal for restaurant menus, shop counters, event posters, business cards and product packaging.
