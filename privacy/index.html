Privacy Policy

Short version: your files never leave your device, we run no accounts, and we do not track you.

Last updated: 7 October 2026.

Your files

Every ToolPixi tool processes files locally in your web browser. When you compress an image, convert a PDF or decode an HEIC photo, the file is read by your browser, processed on your device, and handed back to you as a download. Your files are never uploaded to, transmitted to, or stored on any server operated by us. When you close the tab, nothing remains.

Information you type

Dates, numbers and text you enter into calculators and generators (a date of birth, a paragraph in the word counter, a link in the QR generator) are processed in your browser only. They are not sent to us, saved, or shared.

Accounts and cookies

ToolPixi has no accounts and sets no cookies of its own. We do not use analytics or advertising trackers at this time. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated first, and any future advertising will be disclosed here.

Third-party code libraries

Our pages load a few well-known open-source libraries (such as pdf-lib, PDF.js, heic2any and qrcode-generator) from the public jsDelivr content-delivery network. Loading any file from the internet necessarily reveals your IP address to the network serving it, as with any website; jsDelivr publishes its own privacy practices on its website. These libraries run entirely on your device once loaded and do not send your files anywhere.

External links

Pages may link to other websites (for example, YouTube thumbnail images are fetched from YouTube's image servers when you use that tool). We are not responsible for other sites' privacy practices.

Children

ToolPixi is a general-audience utility site and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children — by design, we collect nothing at all.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with an updated date. Questions about privacy are welcome via the contact page.
