YouTube Thumbnail Downloader

Paste any YouTube link and pull its thumbnail image in every size YouTube offers — including full HD 1280×720.

How to use it

Copy the video link from YouTube (or just its 11-character video ID).
Paste it above and press Get thumbnails.
Download your size — maximum resolution for design work, smaller sizes for quick shares.

Why grab a thumbnail?

Creators study thumbnails that earned millions of clicks; teachers and writers quote them in articles and presentations; channel owners recover their own artwork after losing the source file. The thumbnail is a public image YouTube serves for every video, and this tool simply fetches it for you in the highest quality available — no software, no sign-up, and it never downloads or touches the video itself.
