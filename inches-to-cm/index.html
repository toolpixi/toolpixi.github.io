The formula: cm = inches × 2.54

Multiply the inches by 2.54 — exactly, because the inch is defined as 2.54 centimetres. Twelve inches is 30.48 cm (one foot), 24 inches is 60.96 cm (two feet), and 72 inches is 182.88 cm (six feet). The table above covers the lengths people look up most: screen sizes, heights, paper and fabric.

Screen sizes are the classic case: a "55-inch TV" is measured diagonally, so its screen is 139.7 cm corner to corner — while its width is much less. The same inch-to-cm maths applies to heights: 5 foot 11 is 71 inches, which is 180.34 cm.

The American measurement, made legible to the metric world

Products designed in the United States arrive with their dimensions in inches — the laptop that is 13.3 across, the photo printed at 8×10, the lumber sold as a two-by-four, the baby announced at 21 inches long — and the metric majority of humanity needs those numbers in centimetres before they mean anything. The conversion is exact by definition: one inch is 2.54 centimetres, agreed internationally in 1959, and this page multiplies it precisely, in both directions — because a result you will cut, buy or build from should never rest on a remembered approximation like "about two and a half".

This direction has its own landmark cases. The screen diagonal: a 13-inch laptop is 33 cm, a 27-inch monitor 68.6 cm, a 65-inch television 165 cm corner to corner — the figures that decide furniture and wall mounts. Photographs: the classic 4×6 print is 10×15 cm, 8×10 is 20×25. Heights reverse the usual flow too — an American form's 5′10″ is 178 cm. And fractions are the live edge of this conversion: American measurements arrive as ¾ and ⅞, and the honest centimetre values (1.9 cm, 2.2 cm) are what a metric tape or a machine shop works from — precision here is a courtesy to the person holding the saw.

Conversions are computed on your device and stored nowhere. Inches in, centimetres out, exactly — the way standards are supposed to behave.

Questions, answered

How do I convert inches to cm?

Multiply the inches by 2.54. So 12 inches × 2.54 = 30.48 cm. Type the value above and the converter does it — in either direction.

How tall is 5 foot 11 in cm?

180.34 cm. Five foot eleven is 71 inches, and 71 × 2.54 = 180.34 cm. Six feet is 182.88 cm.

Are TV and laptop screens measured in inches or cm?

Inches, measured diagonally across the screen — a 55-inch TV is 139.7 cm corner to corner. The same conversion applies; just remember the number is the diagonal, not the width.

Is 2.54 cm per inch exact?

Yes — the international inch is defined as exactly 2.54 cm, so multiplying by 2.54 is exact. A converter that shows 2.54 cm for one inch and 30.48 cm for twelve is doing it right.
