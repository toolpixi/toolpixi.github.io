Ramadan 2027: expected to start on 7 February 2027

Ramadan is the ninth month of the Islamic (Hijri) calendar, and 1 Ramadan 1448 AH corresponds to 7 February 2027 in the standard calculated calendar. Because the Islamic year is about 11 days shorter than the solar year, Ramadan moves earlier each Gregorian year — in 2026 it began on 18 February.

Why "expected"? In many countries the month begins with the sighting of the new crescent moon, so the observed start can shift by a day either way depending on the moon and local announcements. Treat 7 February 2027 as the date to plan around, and confirm locally as it approaches. This countdown targets that date using your device clock; after Ramadan begins it rolls to the following year's expected start.
