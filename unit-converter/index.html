How to use it

Pick a category — length, weight, temperature or distance.
Type your value and set the from/to units. Results update as you type.

Built on the exact definitions

Most conversions here are not approximations: since 1959 the inch is defined as exactly 2.54 cm and the pound as exactly 0.45359237 kg, so your answers match official tables. Temperature uses the standard formulas. Whether you are buying fabric in metres for an American pattern, checking a suitcase against a kilogram limit, or reading a Fahrenheit forecast, the number you get is the number that is true.
