Counting down to December 25

Christmas Day falls on December 25 every year, but "how many sleeps?" is a question nobody can answer without counting. This counter does it live: full days remaining, then the hours, minutes and seconds melting away underneath. It uses your own device clock, so it is exact wherever in the world you are — and when December 25 arrives it rolls over and starts counting to next year's Christmas automatically.

Planning around the date? Shopping deadlines, school terms and travel prices all move with it — pair this countdown with our generic Days Until Any Date tool for birthdays, trips and deadlines of your own.
