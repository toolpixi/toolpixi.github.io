Counting down to December 25

Christmas Day falls on December 25 every year, but "how many sleeps?" is a question nobody can answer without counting. This counter does it live: full days remaining, then the hours, minutes and seconds melting away underneath. It uses your own device clock, so it is exact wherever in the world you are — and when December 25 arrives it rolls over and starts counting to next year's Christmas automatically.

Planning around the date? Shopping deadlines, school terms and travel prices all move with it — pair this countdown with our generic Days Until Any Date tool for birthdays, trips and deadlines of your own.

Christmas 2026 falls on a Friday — so this year's countdown ends at the start of a long weekend for most people, with Boxing Day following on the Saturday.

The one countdown that runs the calendar

No other date bends the year around itself like 25 December. Retailers plan their survival by it, schools and offices close for it, postage deadlines, travel prices and family logistics all key off it — and the shoppers' rule is as true as it is ignored: the people who enjoy December are the ones who started in October. A live countdown turns the vague sense of "plenty of time" into a number, which is either a comfort or a spur depending on the month you are reading it in.

The counter at the top of this page ticks in real time — days, hours, minutes and seconds to midnight at the start of Christmas Day — and it is right about the date every year, because Christmas is one of the few fixed points in the calendar: always 25 December, no lunar maths, no movable feast. What does move is the weekday, and the weekday changes the shape of the holiday: a Friday Christmas means a long weekend and a gentle run-up; a Wednesday Christmas splits the week in two and eats annual leave. The countdown page tells you the day-count; planning the leave, the travel day and the last-post deadline around it is the genuinely useful part.

Everything runs in your browser with no sign-up and nothing stored — open the page in December and it is simply there, ticking. Counting down to a different date entirely — a birthday, a wedding, a deadline? The Days Until Any Date counter builds the same live countdown to any day you choose.

Questions, answered

What date is Christmas Day?

December 25, every year. This countdown always targets the next upcoming December 25.

Why does the counter use my device clock?

So the countdown is right in your timezone. If your device clock is wrong, the countdown will be too — check your time settings.

Does it keep working after Christmas?

Yes. On December 26 it starts counting down to next year's Christmas Day.

What day of the week is Christmas 2026?

Christmas Day 2026 falls on a Friday — a long weekend for most people, and the reason this year's countdown is watched so closely.
