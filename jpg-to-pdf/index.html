How to use it

Add your images. Tap the box as many times as you need — photos become pages in the order shown.
Choose a page size. Fit-to-image for scans; A4 for documents you will print or submit.
Create and download your PDF. One file, built on your device in seconds.

Why convert images to PDF at all?

A PDF keeps a set of photos together as one document that looks the same everywhere, prints cleanly, and is what schools, employers, banks and government portals ask for. Sending five loose JPGs invites mix-ups; one PDF named "documents.pdf" does not. Because this tool builds the PDF inside your browser, even sensitive scans — IDs, certificates, contracts — never get uploaded to a stranger's server.
