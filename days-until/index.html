How to use it

Name your event — or leave it blank for a plain countdown.
Pick the date. Any future (or past) date works.
Watch it count down in days, hours, minutes and seconds, on your device's clock.

One countdown for everything

Fixed countdown pages exist for the big shared dates — Christmas, New Year, Ramadan. This one is for yours: the exam you are revising for, the trip you booked months ago, the deadline your team is racing. The counter runs locally and follows your timezone, so the day it celebrates is the day it happens where you are.
