How to use it

Choose your PDF. We will tell you how many pages it has.
Type the pages you want. Ranges like 1-3, single pages like 5, or both together. Semicolons make separate files.
Download your new PDFs. Or split every page into its own file with one click.

When splitting a PDF helps

Only the signed page of a contract needs to go to the bank; only one chapter of a textbook needs printing; a 200-page report is too big to email but the 4-page summary is not. Splitting extracts exactly those pages into fresh PDFs at full original quality. Because it happens in your browser, confidential documents — payslips, medical reports, legal papers — are never uploaded anywhere.
