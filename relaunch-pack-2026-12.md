## 1. Positioning (the one story)

Every free-tools site asks you to upload your files to a stranger's server. ToolPixi doesn't: **106 tools that run 100% in your browser — your files never leave your device.** The extension puts them one click away, adds favourites + recently used, and 4 mini-tools that work fully offline.

**Description:**
Most free tool sites make you upload your PDF, your photo, your data — to a server you'll never see.

ToolPixi is built the other way around. All 106 tools run entirely in your browser: converters, PDF tools, calculators, generators, text tools. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is stored, no account exists to sign up for.

The extension makes it instant:
- 🔎 Search and launch any of the 106 tools from the toolbar
- ⭐ Favourites + recently used, saved locally in your browser
- 🧰 4 built-in mini-tools that work 100% offline: password generator, word counter, Base64 encoder/decoder, unit converter
- 🖱️ Right-click selected text to count its words

Permissions: storage, context menus, tabs. That's all. No tracking, no analytics, no data collection — declared "none" in the manifest itself.

Free forever. If a tool ever needs your file on a server, it doesn't belong on ToolPixi.
