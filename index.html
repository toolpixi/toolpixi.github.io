Most tool sites make you create an account, wait for a server, and hand over your files. ToolPixi is built the other way: every tool runs on your own device, in the tab you already have open. We started with the jobs people search for every day: shrinking a photo to the exact KB size an application form demands, converting an iPhone HEIC photo so it opens anywhere, merging PDFs before an email, and the date maths nobody wants to do by hand.
