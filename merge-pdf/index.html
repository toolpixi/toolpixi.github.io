Merging without the usual traps

Most "free" PDF mergers make you wait in a queue, stamp a watermark, or ask for your email before the download. ToolPixi merges on your own device instead: your documents are never uploaded, there is nothing to sign up for, and the output is a clean PDF with every page in its original quality. It is the same technology (pdf-lib) that professional tools use, running locally in your browser tab.
