The formula: °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9

Subtract 32 from the Fahrenheit temperature, then multiply by 5/9. The 32 accounts for the different starting points — water freezes at 32 °F but 0 °C — and the 5/9 corrects for the different step sizes. So 77 °F becomes (77 − 32) × 5/9 = 45 × 5/9 = 25 °C. Room temperature, 72 °F, is 22.2 °C, and body temperature, 98.6 °F, is 37 °C.

Most of the world reads weather in Celsius, so this is the conversion travellers use most: a 68 °F day in New York is a 20 °C day in London, and a 90 °F forecast is 32.2 °C — genuinely hot by either measure.

Translating American weather into the world's scale

The United States reports its world in Fahrenheit — its forecasts, its ovens, its fevers — and the rest of the planet, plus all of science, reads Celsius. If your instincts are metric, an American forecast is a foreign language with familiar-looking numbers, which is the dangerous kind: 90 sounds mild and is a fierce 32°C; 32 sounds like a warm day in Celsius terms and is literally freezing. This converter applies the exact formula, °C = (°F − 32) × 5⁄9, in both directions, and its table builds the instinct: 32°F = 0°C where water freezes, 212°F = 100°C where it boils, 98.6°F = 37°C where you run, and −40 is −40 — the scales' one meeting point.

Two domains dominate this direction. Weather, where the feel of the numbers matters more than their precision: 50°F is a jacketed 10°C, 68°F a perfect 20°C, 86°F a hot 30°C, and a fever of 101°F is a concerning 38.3°C — the medical conversion being the one to get right at 3 a.m. with a thermometer in hand. And cooking, travelling the other way into metric kitchens: the American recipe's 350°F is 175–177°C, gas mark 4. For the metric-to-American journey, the twin Celsius to Fahrenheit page is the specialist; this one exists for the American number in front of a metric reader.

Questions, answered

What is the formula to convert Fahrenheit to Celsius?

Subtract 32 from the Fahrenheit temperature, then multiply by 5/9: °C = (°F − 32) × 5/9. So 77 °F becomes (77 − 32) × 5/9 = 25 °C.

What is 72 degrees Fahrenheit in Celsius?

22.2 °C — a comfortable room temperature. 68 °F is 20 °C, 77 °F is 25 °C, and 86 °F is 30 °C.

Which countries still use Fahrenheit?

The United States, the Bahamas, Belize, the Cayman Islands and Palau. Almost every other country uses Celsius, which is why this direction of the conversion is the one travellers need.

What is freezing point in Fahrenheit?

32 °F — the same as 0 °C. Water boils at 212 °F (100 °C), and body temperature is 98.6 °F (37 °C).
