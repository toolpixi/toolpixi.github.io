How to use it

Made for strict upload limits

Job portals, universities, banks and government sites routinely cap PDF uploads at 200KB or even 100KB — while a scanned document from a phone is easily 5–15MB. This tool closes that gap: it re-renders each page at the highest quality that fits your target, stepping down only as far as needed. If your PDF simply cannot go that small without turning unreadable, we say so and still give you the smallest clean result, instead of a blurry file that gets rejected anyway.
