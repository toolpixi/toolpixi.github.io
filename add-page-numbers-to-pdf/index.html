Add Page Numbers to PDF

Stamp page numbers on every page of a PDF — position, format and start number are yours to choose. Free, instant, private.

Private by design: this tool runs entirely in your browser. Your files are never uploaded to a server, and nothing is stored.
Tap to choose a PDF — or drop it here
One PDF at a time

How to use it

Choose a PDF. Tap the box above or drag one PDF into it.
Pick your style. Set the position, number format, start number, and whether the first page stays unnumbered.
Download. Press “Add page numbers”, then download your stamped PDF.

Why number your PDF pages?

Page numbers turn a stack of pages into a document you can actually navigate: readers can find their place after printing, you can point someone to “page 14” in an email, and numbered pages are expected in reports, theses, contracts and legal filings.

This tool stamps the numbers directly into the PDF in your browser — the file never leaves your device, so private contracts and unpublished manuscripts stay private. Every page is copied exactly as it was; only the numbers are added.

Related tools

Merge PDF — Combine several PDFs into one file, in your order. Split PDF — Pull out page ranges or save every page as its own PDF. Rotate PDF — Turn scanned pages upright — rotate a whole PDF 90°, 180° or 270°. JPG to PDF — Turn one or many photos into a single PDF document. PDF to JPG — Convert each PDF page into a crisp JPG image. Compress PDF — Shrink a PDF toward 200KB or 100KB for upload forms.
