Number to Words Converter

Turn a number into English words — the way cheques, contracts, invoices and exam forms ask for it.

—

How to use it

Type the number — digits only, up to just under a trillion.
Pick the system — international (million/billion) or Indian (lakh/crore).
Copy the words straight onto the cheque, invoice or form.

Two systems, one number

Most of the world groups big numbers in thousands, millions and billions. South Asia groups them differently — by lakh and crore — and writes them with the commas in different places too. The same figure, both ways:

NumberInternationalIndian
100,000One hundred thousandOne lakh
1,000,000One millionTen lakh
10,000,000Ten millionOne crore
1,000,000,000One billionOne hundred crore

Cheques are where this matters most: a bank in Mumbai expects lakh and crore; an invoice to New York expects millions. Writing the wrong system's words on a cheque is a classic reason for it to be returned — pick the system your document will be read in, and copy the words exactly.
