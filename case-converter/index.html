Case Converter

Paste text in any case and convert it — UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, Sentence case, aLtErNaTiNg or iNVERTED — with one tap.

How to use it

Type or paste your text.
Tap a case style. The result updates instantly, with a live word count.
Copy the result and paste it wherever it needs to go.

The caps-lock rescue, and four other everyday jobs

Everyone has received the paragraph typed with caps lock stuck on. Retyping is nobody's plan A: Sentence case fixes it in one tap. Title Case formats headlines, video titles and product names; UPPERCASE suits labels and warnings; aLtErNaTiNg and iNVERTED are the playful cases social posts run on. The converter runs locally and counts words as it goes, so it doubles as a quick length check before you post.

Case is formatting, and formatting is meaning

The same words in different case are different artefacts. UPPER CASE is a shout and a headline convention; Title Case is the shape of book titles, headlines and proper names; Sentence case is how prose breathes; lower case is the dialect of code, URLs and filenames — and retyping a passage to move between them is the kind of chore that introduces errors precisely because it is mindless. This converter transforms a whole text between the standard cases in one tap: paste it in mangled, take it out shaped, with every word handled consistently — including the edge cases humans botch, like the small words (a, the, of) that headline style keeps lower.

The cases earn their keep in different worlds. Writers and editors move between Title Case headlines and sentence-case subheads all day. Developers live in the stricter dialects — camelCase, snake_case, kebab-case — where case is not style but syntax, and a variable renamed wrongly is a bug. Social media and design work has its own conventions, and the all-caps announcement still has its uses provided it is a choice rather than a caps-lock accident. The unifying truth: readers judge correctness by consistency, and a text whose case is uniformly, deliberately one thing reads as professional before its first sentence is understood.

Your text is transformed in your browser, character by character, and never leaves your device — drafts, manuscripts and internal documents get reformatted without being uploaded anywhere.

A note from the research on all-caps, since it is the conversion most often requested: text set entirely in capitals is measurably slower to read — roughly a tenth slower in classic legibility studies — because word shapes flatten into uniform rectangles. Capitals are for labels, warnings and single lines of emphasis. Anything longer that arrives in shout-case is a candidate for Sentence case and a calmer page, which is most of what this converter is ever asked to do.

Questions, answered

What is Title Case?

The first letter of every word is capitalised and the rest are lower case — the style used for headlines and book titles, like 'The Quick Brown Fox'.

What is Sentence case?

Only the first letter of each sentence is capitalised, exactly like ordinary writing. It is the quickest way to rescue a paragraph typed in ALL CAPS.

Why does my ALL CAPS text need rescuing?

Text typed with caps lock on reads as shouting and is hard to scan. Convert it to Sentence case here instead of retyping it — a classic two-second fix.

Is my text uploaded or saved?

No. Conversion happens in your browser as you type; your text never leaves the page.
